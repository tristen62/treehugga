The tree hugga method

Grip. Cut. Lower.

Most trees come down by felling or by being cut apart piece by piece, and both can damage what's below. The tree hugga takes the tree down from the top while it's still standing.

01

Grip

The machine clamps the trunk firmly, so the tree can't swing or drop.

02

Cut

A blade cuts through the trunk while the tree is still held in the grip.

03

Lower

The top is lowered to the ground in a controlled way, clear of buildings, fences and gardens.

About TreeHugga

Meet Ray, the man behind the machine.

TreeHugga Tree Services is run by Ray Monk, who has worked with trees for twenty years. Ray's engineering background means he knows machinery inside out, so the gear is kept running smoothly and jobs run to plan.

His tree hugga system takes trees down from the top while they're still standing. There's no felling and no chopping the tree up piece by piece, which is what usually damages fences, gardens and buildings.

Today TreeHugga is a preferred supplier for North Canterbury local authorities, with health and safety requirements in place, and also runs Canterbury Greenwaste Processing, home of The Beast.

What size trees can the tree hugga remove?

Trees up to 400mm in trunk diameter and 8m high. For bigger trees, call us and we'll talk through the options, including The Beast for larger-scale work.

Do you remove the stump as well?

Yes. We offer stump grinding and removal, plus root raking. You can have a tree removed and the stump left in place, but we don't recommend it.

What happens to the branches and debris?

You choose. We can mulch it on site with The Beast for use in your landscaping, or remove it all and clean up. It's best to decide before the job, so give us a call or tell us on the quote form.

Do you prune trees?

We top trees, but we don't prune them, and we're not arborists. For delicate pruning or tree health advice you'll want a qualified arborist. For removal, topping, hedges and clearing, we're your people.

What about trees near power lines?

We work with Orion and Mainpower when operating near the power supply, so the job is done safely and by the book.

When can a tree be removed, and what does it cost?

Removal can happen any time of year, though it's more efficient when the ground is firm. Every job is different, so we quote each one for free. Photos make it faster. Some trees may be protected, so check with your council if you're unsure.
